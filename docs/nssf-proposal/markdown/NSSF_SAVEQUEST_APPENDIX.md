## Mandatory Contributions

- Employee: **5%** of gross monthly salary
- Employer: **10%** of employee gross
- Total: **15%**

---

## Benefit Types

1. Age benefit (55+)
2. Withdrawal benefit (50+, out of employment 1 year)
3. Invalidity benefit
4. Survivors benefit
5. Exempted employment benefit
6. Midterm access (45+, 10+ years contributions)
7. Emigration grant
