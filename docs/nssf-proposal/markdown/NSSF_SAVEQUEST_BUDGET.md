## A.1 Executive Budget Summary

This budget covers adaptation of a **proven Telegram Mini App engagement platform** (currently deployed as URAPearls for Uganda Revenue Authority) into **NSSF SaveQuest** — a gamified member engagement, financial literacy, and voluntary savings activation platform aligned with **Vision 2035 (50:50:95)**.

| Phase | Duration | Budget (UGX) | Budget (USD)* |
|-------|----------|--------------|---------------|
| **Phase 0** — Discovery & Co-design | 6 weeks | 85,000,000 | 23,000 |
| **Phase 1** — Interactive Prototype (POC) | 10 weeks | 195,000,000 | 53,000 |
| **Phase 2** — Full Platform Adaptation | 20 weeks | 520,000,000 | 140,000 |
| **Phase 3** — NSSF Systems Integration | 12 weeks | 280,000,000 | 76,000 |
| **Phase 4** — Content & Financial Literacy Curriculum | 16 weeks | 185,000,000 | 50,000 |
| **Phase 5** — Pilot (3 regions) | 12 weeks | 145,000,000 | 39,000 |
| **Phase 6** — National Rollout & Change Management | 8 weeks | 165,000,000 | 45,000 |
| **Year 1 Operations & Support** | 12 months | 220,000,000 | 59,000 |
| **Contingency (12%)** | — | 223,200,000 | 60,000 |
| **TOTAL — Year 1 Programme** | **~18 months** | **2,018,200,000** | **~545,000** |
| **Year 2–3 Annual Support & Enhancement** | per year | 320,000,000 / yr | 86,000 / yr |
