NSSF SaveQuest — EXCO Summary (1 Page)

Prepared for: National Social Security Fund (NSSF) Uganda | www.nssfug.org
Date: June 2026 | Classification: Confidential — EXCO Review

The Ask

Approve Option B — Pilot Programme (UGX 1.41 Billion / ~USD 381,000) to validate NSSF SaveQuest, a gamified Telegram engagement platform that drives financial literacy, Smartlife Flexi enrollment, and member referrals — aligned to Vision 2035 (50:50:95).

The Problem

Gap Impact
Only 11% of working Ugandans covered (3.35M of ~30M labour force) Missing 2035 target of 15M members
98% of beneficiaries spend benefits within 2 years Retirement poverty persists despite savings
85% of gig workers have no retirement savings Informal sector remains underserved
NSSF Go handles transactions but not daily engagement Smartlife Flexi needs habit-forming activation

The Solution

NSSF SaveQuest — adapt a proven engagement platform (URAPearls, already in production for URA) into NSSF's digital ecosystem:

  • Telegram Mini App — no app-store install; reaches informal workers
  • 5 modules: Home, Learn, Services, Earn, Saver Network (Guild)
  • Shield Points — non-monetary gamification (White / Blue / Gold tiers)
  • Integrates with: NSSF Go, Sanyu chatbot, Smartlife Flexi, Financial Literacy Programme
  • Does NOT replace NSSF Go for payments, claims, or e-statements

Strategic Alignment (Vision 2035)

Target SaveQuest Contribution
50% coverage Referral engine, informal sector onboarding via Telegram
UGX 50T AUM Smartlife Flexi habit formation, voluntary top-ups
95% satisfaction Gamified learning, 24/7 Help, reduced branch visits
