NSSF SaveQuest — Part C: Business Plan

Prepared for: National Social Security Fund (NSSF) Uganda
Date: June 2026 | Classification: Confidential

C.1 Executive Summary

NSSF SaveQuest transforms NSSF from transactional pension administrator to daily financial companion for Ugandan workers — supporting Vision 2035 (50% coverage, UGX 50T AUM, 95% satisfaction).

Built by adapting a production-proven platform (433 files, 139 APIs, 26 admin modules), delivery in 18 months at ~UGX 2.0B — 43–60% below greenfield cost.

ROI drivers: Smartlife enrollment; contact centre deflection; referral coverage expansion; financial literacy → benefit retention; employer compliance awareness.

Recommendation: Option B — Pilot Programme (UGX 1.41B).

C.8 Technology & Security
